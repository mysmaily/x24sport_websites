# PND Sport V2: Arena Campaign

Source: `frontend-design`.

## Direction

Bold sports retail campaign with controlled density. The memorable device is an
orange diagonal hero paired with a dark arena image, while product browsing
remains conventional and fast.

- Dominant: charcoal `#111316`
- Accent: PND orange `#ff5526`
- Catalog background: cool gray `#eceeea`
- Shape rule: alternating corners on campaign surfaces, restrained square grid
- Motion: one short hero entrance sequence plus hover feedback
- Type: existing condensed PND heading stack, not a new dependency

## Preserve

- Current information architecture, copy, routes and primary CTAs
- Starting price treatment and product-first catalog
- 44px mobile targets, focus styles and reduced-motion fallback

## Avoid

- Pattern Room, neon lime, blueprint elements and hard offset shadows
- Purple gradients, glass cards and oversized display type
- Decorative animation that competes with products
