# Mayaodongphuc.com.vn Blog Plan

Created: 2026-08-20
Planning window: 2026-08-21 to 2026-10-20
Tenant: `mayaodongphuc`
Domain: `mayaodongphuc.com.vn`

## Goal

Build a long-term Vietnamese SEO content engine for made-to-order uniforms. The
first two months should make the site feel active, cover the highest-intent
buyer questions, and create enough internal content depth to support future
product/category pages.

The plan is not to copy competitor articles. Competitor pages are used only to
identify search intent, content structure, and missing angles.

## Market Notes

Competitors with strong content tend to cluster around:

- Large sample galleries: "99+ mau", "200+ mau", "999+ mau".
- Fabric education: cotton, ca sau, poly, thun lanh, thun me, dry-fit,
  bamboo, kate, kaki.
- Process and buyer trust: consultation, design mockup, sample approval,
  production, quality check, delivery.
- Industry pages: company, restaurant, cafe, hotel, spa, healthcare, school,
  factory, construction, logistics, event, team building.
- Commercial modifiers: bang gia, gia xuong, so luong it, giao nhanh, thiet
  ke mien phi, in theu logo.

Useful competitor references:

- Dong Phuc Gau: strong sample-led pages for company polo, T-shirt, team
  building, and industry contexts.
- Dong Phuc Hai Anh: strong process and polo/fabric pages with many commercial
  modifiers.
- Dong Phuc Tam An: good comparison-style fabric content and technical
  explanations.
- Dong Phuc Ong Vang and May Do: strong restaurant, cafe, hotel, healthcare,
  and service-uniform pages.
- Gạo House: strong broad pages for corporate T-shirts and polo uniforms.

Opportunity for Mayaodongphuc:

- Be more practical and decision-oriented than generic sample galleries.
- Use "quote-ready" content: every post helps the buyer prepare logo, quantity,
  size, fabric, use context, and deadline.
- Avoid unsupported price claims until commercial terms are verified.
- Build around "uniform systems" for organizations, not one-off retail shirts.

## Publishing Cadence

Phase 0, production setup: 2026-08-21 to 2026-08-23

- Produce first 14 article drafts and cover prompts.
- Prepare reusable article template.
- No need to publish during this setup window unless CMS capacity is ready.

Phase 1, foundation sprint: 2026-08-24 to 2026-09-06

- Publish 1 article per day for 14 days.
- Purpose: make the blog indexable and useful quickly.

Phase 2, steady cadence: 2026-09-07 to 2026-10-18

- Publish 4 articles per week: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday.
- Purpose: preserve consistency while leaving time for editing and image
  generation.

Phase 3, review and adjustment: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-20

- Publish 2 conversion-focused posts.
- Review Search Console impressions, indexed pages, and internal links.

Total first-window output: 40 published articles.

## Editorial Rules

Every article must include:

- Search-focused title.
- Slug in Vietnamese without diacritics.
- Meta description around 140-160 characters.
- First paragraph that answers the search intent directly.
- 5-8 H2 sections.
- Practical buyer checklist.
- FAQ section with 3-5 questions.
- CTA: "Gui logo, so luong, mau ao tham khao va thoi han can hang de duoc tu
  van cau hinh phu hop."
- 3-6 internal links to related blog/product/category pages.
- Image brief for one 1200x630 cover and one supporting image.

Avoid:

- Exact competitor phrasing.
- Unsupported fixed price tables.
- Claims about delivery time, warranty, discount, or minimum quantity unless
  verified by the business.
- Thin listicles that only repeat "dep, chuyen nghiep, gia re".

## Production Workflow

Batch rhythm:

1. Create 20 article briefs at a time.
2. Draft 10 articles.
3. Generate or select covers for those 10.
4. Human review for factual and commercial safety.
5. Upload to CMS as drafts.
6. Schedule according to the calendar.
7. Every two weeks, update internal links from newly published posts.
8. After 60 days, review Search Console and choose the next 40 topics based on
   impressions and buyer intent.

Quality gate before publishing:

- No copied paragraphs.
- No price claim unless approved.
- CTA exists.
- At least 3 internal links.
- Image has descriptive alt text.
- Title and meta are unique.
- Article answers the reader's actual decision.
