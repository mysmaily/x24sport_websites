# Vietnamese market requirements

Apply these requirements to a tenant only where they fit the verified business
model and target customers. They are design and content constraints, not a
license to invent local-business facts.

## Buyer clarity before visual novelty

- Make the next buyer action obvious: browse products, request a quotation,
  send a design, get size advice, or contact the approved channel. Do not make
  users decode a foreign-style marketing journey before they can act.
- Present real product identity, images, price or quote-price state, variants,
  quantity/customization conditions, and decision help before decorative brand
  storytelling. A B2B/teamwear buyer often needs consultation; that does not
  justify hiding catalog information.
- Keep mobile commerce compact. At 390x844, primary navigation, product
  discovery, decision information, and CTA must remain usable without accidental
  blank space, overlap, clipping, or horizontal page scroll.

## Vietnamese language and information conventions

- Write natural, concise Vietnamese for the intended buyer. Preserve correct
  diacritics; do not publish machine-like literal translations, unexplained
  English labels, keyword lists, or inflated sales language.
- Use the terms customers actually use only after checking product/domain facts.
  Keep names for products, fabrics, sport positions, printing methods, and size
  conventions consistent across category, product, guide, metadata, and CTA.
- Use `₫`/`VNĐ`, `cm`, `kg`, quantities, and dates in a consistent Vietnamese
  convention when these facts are present. Do not format or imply prices, sale
  periods, payment methods, delivery times, or stock that have not been verified.
- Use Vietnamese-friendly ASCII slugs with stable meaning. Avoid English-only
  paths when Vietnamese is the customer-facing language unless a verified brand
  or product term requires it.

## Trust and conversion

- Show only verified trust signals: legal business identity, address/showroom,
  production capability, contact channel, payment/order guidance, policies,
  project examples, reviews, or certifications. Omit rather than fabricate.
- Make quote/customization steps concrete when they are real: what a customer
  provides, what information the business returns, and the approved next action.
  Do not promise a turnaround or design service without a factual source.
- Contacts, policies, price state, product availability, and fulfillment claims
  must agree across rendered copy, CMS, schema, and transaction behavior.

## Taxonomy and Vietnamese search intent

- Organize around durable product families and real buyer intents such as sport,
  garment type, team/company/school use case, customization need, or buying
  guide. A keyword becomes a landing page only when it has distinct content,
  products or service guidance, and a conversion purpose.
- Location pages require a real local service distinction or factual local data.
  Do not create province/district pages merely by replacing a place name.
- Treat colour, size, material, gender, and similar facets as filters unless a
  curated page has independent buyer value and an approved indexation policy.
- Link category pages to relevant guides, product pages to decision help, and
  guides back to the next shopping or contact action. Avoid orphan content and
  bulk keyword pages.

## Required market-fit review

Before launch, inspect representative mobile and desktop screens with realistic
Vietnamese content. Confirm language, currency/unit format where shown, readable
text/contrast, familiar action labels, truthful trust signals, functional contact
journey, and that product/quote information appears before nonessential visual
decoration. Record the evidence or a factual blocker in the dossier.
