# Shot Direction

## Default Casting

- Vietnamese/Southeast Asian adults, about 20-35.
- Default group size: 4-5 people.
- Allowed range: 3-8 people.
- Use mixed gender unless the user specifies men, women, family, students, or staff.
- Vary height, build, hairstyle, and pose; avoid cloned faces and mirrored poses.

## Composition

- Use head-to-mid-thigh or head-to-knee framing for most images.
- Keep shirt fronts visible on at least half the group.
- If the input includes front/back designs, turn 1-2 people naturally to show the back.
- Keep the camera close enough that collars, sleeves, chest art, and fabric texture are inspectable.
- Avoid wide full-body scenes where grass, shoes, or props become more important than shirts.

## Outdoor Scenes

- Park picnic: picnic mat, light baskets, soft trees, relaxed standing/sitting mix.
- Company outing: lawn, simple team-building props, walking or casual cheering.
- Resort garden: bright clean greenery, paved path, outdoor seating, vacation-team mood.
- Campsite picnic: tent or picnic table in the background only, not blocking shirts.
- Walking group: 3-5 people moving toward camera, natural laughter, shirt fronts readable.

## Overlay Safe Zones

- Reserve one top corner for the logo.
- Reserve a quiet side or lower edge for features.
- Do not cover faces, hair, hands, collars, main chest artwork, back artwork, or customer marks.
- Feature overlays may cover tiny areas of plain sleeve or side torso on secondary people only when the design remains clear.
