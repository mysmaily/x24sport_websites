Female football 9:16 style rules:

- Use a full-body or near full-body vertical composition with shoes visible and floor space below the feet.
- Keep the camera around waist/chest height with a mild telephoto feel; avoid top-down framing that compresses the legs.
- Make the model read as a naturally tall Vietnamese adult female, roughly `165-170 cm`, with balanced longer legs, realistic knees, calves, ankles, and shoes.
- Keep the jersey hem around the upper hip. Do not make the shirt so oversized or long that it visually shortens the legs.
- Pair the jersey with simple black athletic shorts unless the user requests another bottom.
- Use a relaxed standing pose with one leg subtly forward or angled to lengthen the silhouette naturally.
- Preserve the approved X24Sport shop feel: warm indoor light, cream walls, glossy beige tile, black racks, and secondary hanging sportswear.
- Reject outputs where the torso looks oversized, the legs look short/compressed, shoes float, or knees/calves look distorted.
