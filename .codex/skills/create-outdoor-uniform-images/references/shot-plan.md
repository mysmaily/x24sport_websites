# Default five-image website set

Use this plan when the user asks for a set but does not define each shot. Adapt locations and activities to the garment while keeping one coherent campaign.

| Slot | Role | Default ratio | Direction |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Catalog hero | 1:1 | Randomly use 3–7 adults in a park/picnic setting; use a head-to-mid-thigh or head-to-knee crop with clear shirt fronts; choose a naturally balanced loose arc, diagonal, staggered cluster, or layered formation. |
| 2 | Landscape banner | 16:9 or 3:2 | Randomly use a different count from 3–7, walking or interacting naturally at a company outing; retain modest background breathing room for small cards. |
| 3 | Portrait social/catalog | 4:5 | Randomly use a different count from 3–7 in a lively stacked or editorial composition; favor three-quarter bodies and shirt visibility over shoes. |
| 4 | Clean comparison | 4:5 or 3:2 | Randomly use a different count from 3–7 against a simple outdoor wall or soft studio-like daylight; low visual noise. |
| 5 | Candid/detail | 1:1 | Randomly use a different count from 3–7 preparing a picnic or team activity; closer view of fabric, collar, print, and expressions. |

## Casting

- Default to Vietnamese/Southeast Asian adults aged approximately 20–35.
- Use a balanced mixed-gender cast unless the request specifies women, men, family, school, or another group.
- Make faces individually recognizable, attractive, warm, and non-identical.
- Vary height, build, hairstyle, and pose subtly; keep styling commercially cohesive.
- Reuse a coherent casting direction, but do not promise exact identity continuity unless a face reference is supplied and supported.
- Vary group size across 3–7 people, formation, pose mix, camera distance, and gaze. Avoid repeating a fixed five-person triangular composition.
- Every garment must have practical sleeves. Convert sleeveless references to short sleeves while preserving torso artwork.

## Outdoor art direction

- Favor real daylight, soft sun, open shade, green lawn, picnic mat, resort garden, light team-building props, and uncluttered backgrounds.
- Keep exposure realistic. Avoid nuclear greens, clipped white shirts, orange skin, fantasy sunsets, excessive HDR, and oversaturated skies.
- Favor 35–70 mm photographic perspective. Use believable depth of field without cutting sharp halos around hair.
- Make body language friendly and spontaneous: walking, laughing, arranging picnic items, leaning naturally, or light team activity.
- Avoid rows of rigid catalog poses unless the user requests a lineup.
- Keep hero framing shirt-first. Reduce lower-leg, shoe, and empty-lawn area; use full-body framing only as deliberate supporting variety.
- Keep the group composition natural and visually balanced; do not push models to one side for graphics. Prefer small quiet background pockets near a corner for the vertical logo, feature cards, and understated hotline; never place them over a face or shirt.

## Children variant

When children are explicitly requested, replace the adult cast completely or use the exact family/teacher mix requested. Use play, picnic, school outing, nature discovery, or supervised team activity. Keep shorts/skirts practical, poses active but safe, expressions natural, and camera framing neutral and age-appropriate.

## One or several garment designs

- One design: repeat it consistently across the group; size and natural wrinkles may vary.
- Coordinated colorways: assign them intentionally and balance colors across the composition.
- Front/back pair: place some people front-facing and a smaller number naturally turned to show the back.
- Different designs: label A/B/C in the prompt and state exactly which people wear each; never blend design features.
