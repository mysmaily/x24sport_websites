# Quality gates

Use this rubric for every proof and final image. A single hard failure rejects the image.

## 1. Design fidelity — hard gate

- Match the garment silhouette, collar, sleeve length, cuffs, panels, and hem.
- Match every major color and boundary; reject hue drift that changes the product.
- Match artwork/logo location, orientation, relative scale, and count.
- Keep important readable text correctly spelled. Reject mirrored, scrambled, duplicated, or invented text.
- Keep the correct front/back design on each visible side.
- Keep one design consistent across all wearers unless the brief assigns variants.
- Preserve approved customer/team artwork that is part of the exact garment design. Reject source-vendor marks, photographer watermarks, URLs, corner logos, or unrequested brand additions.
- Require practical sleeves on every shirt. Reject tank tops, sleeveless jerseys, cap sleeves exposing the armpit, or oversized armholes. For sleeveless inputs, accept only a faithful short-sleeve conversion.

## 2. People and age — hard gate

- Require distinct, commercially suitable, believable Vietnamese/Southeast Asian faces rather than cloned identities.
- Require natural skin texture, pores, fine lines, asymmetry, hair strands, moist but not glassy eyes, and plausible teeth.
- Reject waxy skin, porcelain blur, face-filter smoothness, doll eyes, melted ears, repeated facial features, or uncanny smiles.
- Inspect every hand, finger, arm, leg, foot, and joint. Reject missing, fused, extra, twisted, or implausibly occluded anatomy.
- Require plausible scale, perspective, gaze, physical contact, balance, and weight.
- Match the requested age group. Never substitute children for adults or adults for children.
- For children, require age-appropriate proportions, clothing, pose, activity, makeup, and framing.

## 3. Garment realism — hard gate

- Require recognizable cotton jersey or performance-knit texture appropriate to the design.
- Require seams, collar rib, sleeve seam, hem stitching, thickness, gravity, and natural wrinkles.
- Make the print follow folds and body curvature without appearing pasted on or melting into the fabric.
- Reject plastic shine, rubber surfaces, metallic cloth unless specified, flat vector fabric, excessive edge sharpness, or painted-on shadows.
- Keep fit plausible across different bodies without changing the product cut.

## 4. Scene and group realism — hard gate

- Require one coherent light direction, color temperature, shadow softness, and contact shadow.
- Ground every person on the same plane; reject floating feet, intersecting bodies, impossible overlaps, and mismatched scale.
- Use varied but coordinated expressions and poses; reject cloned poses and everyone staring identically at camera.
- Keep props secondary, anatomically usable, and free of malformed packaging or random readable brands.
- Require real photographic depth, lens perspective, and background detail; reject synthetic blur halos or cutout edges.

## 5. Commerce and composition — hard gate

- Show the design clearly on enough people to sell the uniform.
- For hero and primary catalog images, make collars, sleeves, chest artwork, fabric texture, and torso fit visually dominant. Prefer head-to-mid-thigh or head-to-knee framing; reject compositions where lower legs, shoes, or empty ground consume more attention than the shirts.
- Keep full-body views as supporting variants unless specifically requested. Reject awkward crops directly through knees, ankles, elbows, or other joints.
- Keep faces and garments away from unsafe crop edges and website text-safe zones when requested.
- Avoid wardrobe or props that compete with shirt colors and artwork.
- Reject unintended captions, random typography, watermarks, signatures, logos, or borders.
- Match the requested aspect ratio and visual role.
- Require believable group counts in each accepted scene image. For the catalog, verify the hero and two supporting windows vary formation, crop, scale, or context instead of repeating the exact same crop.
- Require exactly one bundled Mayaodongphuc logo in the left/top information zone, undistorted, fully visible, and pinned to the top-left or top-right image corner as a fixed brand mark. Reject logos placed inside the feature rail, middle overlay stack, bottom band, garment area, or over a face, hair, hand, collar, approved garment artwork, customer/team mark, or important action. The feature cards may overlap noncritical plain fabric on one or two secondary shirts when the design remains clear.
- Require the Mayaodongphuc name and mark to be immediately recognizable at normal viewing size. Target roughly 12–14% of the shorter image edge; reject a tiny unreadable logo or an oversized mark that competes with the garments.
- Reject any Mayaodongphuc corner logo printed onto the garment itself.
- Require deliberate graphic design: clear hierarchy, aligned grid, consistent icon family, balanced margins, readable spacing, scanned quiet-region placement, automatic dark/light text contrast based on the sampled placement region, and restrained brand accents. Prefer icon + text lockups on natural paint swashes, ink-like washes, or diagonal gradient accents; when those treatments look unpolished, use a clean evenly rounded translucent shape. Rail layouts must use the image edge as the text alignment axis: right-side rails right-align text close to the right edge, and left-side rails start text close to the left edge. Feature text must remain readable when viewed as a catalog thumbnail. Reject visible text outlines/borders, heavy opaque panels, cluttered badge strips, generic sidebar blocks, or any overlay that forces the models to one side.
- Require exactly four supported feature categories—fabric, design, durability, and printing—with concise Vietnamese copy and one consistent outline icon per category.
- Require one small phone-call icon beside the exact hotline `0989 353 247`. Keep it typographically subordinate to the garment features; reject missing digits, added digits, malformed spacing, any second phone number, or CTA-like treatment.
- Keep the combined logo, feature callouts, and hotline visually light and secondary to the garment. Reject unsupported technical claims, crowded badges, scattered labels without alignment, oversized overlays, inconsistent icons, weak contrast, or feature text printed onto a shirt. Do not reject a polished overlay solely because it covers a small area of plain sleeve or torso fabric away from the main design.
- Require bundled logo pixels to remain crisp, undistorted, correctly spelled, and identical across a set. Reject AI-redrawn versions or generated approximations of the logo assets.

## Acceptance score

After all hard gates pass, score each dimension from 0–2:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design | wrong | minor noncritical drift | faithful |
| Faces/anatomy | obvious AI defect | slightly stiff | natural |
| Fabric/print | plastic or pasted | plausible | tactile and integrated |
| Scene/group | incoherent | usable | candid and coherent |
| Commerce | weak product view | usable | strong catalog image |

Accept at 9/10 or 10/10 only. A score never overrides a hard-gate failure.

## Targeted corrections

Correct only the failing dimension in an iteration:

- Design: “Keep the accepted people, pose, setting, lighting, and crop. Correct only the shirt to match [specific collar/panel/artwork detail].”
- Face: “Keep the accepted garment and composition. Replace the waxy facial treatment with natural skin texture, facial asymmetry, lively eyes, and plausible teeth.”
- Hands: “Keep everything else. Repair only [person/location] hand to five anatomically correct fingers with natural grip and occlusion.”
- Fabric: “Keep the design unchanged. Add subtle [cotton/performance-knit] weave, seam thickness, hem stitching, gravity wrinkles, and print deformation following the cloth.”
- Consistency: “Keep the accepted design and cast. Make all shirts use the identical approved color blocking and artwork placement.”

Inspect the corrected image again from gate 1; edits can regress previously accepted details.


## Shirt typography — hard gate

When a slogan/text graphic is requested, treat it as approved garment artwork, not a caption. Require exact spelling and deliberate apparel typography: clear hierarchy, intentional tracking/leading, optical centering, controlled print scale, palette-aware ink colors, and a composition appropriate to the shirt panels. The print must follow torso perspective, cloth curvature, wrinkles, lighting, texture, and occlusion. Reject plain default-font typing, floating text, rectangular text boxes, malformed letters, extra words, inconsistent line breaks, random decorative marks, or materially different lockups across models.

For corrections, preserve the accepted person, pose, scene, garment construction, colors, and lighting; edit only the shirt graphic. If a large group makes exact lettering unreliable, favor 3–4 models or fewer prominently front-facing readable prints rather than accepting gibberish. See `shirt-typography.md`.


## 6. V4 board composition — hard gate

- Final default deliverable is exactly two accepted scene images plus one landscape 3:2 catalog board, not the old two-square set and not three separate generated scene images.
- Require one dominant hero plus two supporting scene windows unless the user explicitly requests another layout.
- Require a pale/translucent garment-color information zone that fades into the hero; reject a generic heavy opaque sidebar.
- Require the campaign slogan in the information zone with clear hierarchy and enough negative space.
- Require four feature explanations in the left zone and a compact repeated four-feature summary in the footer.
- Require a deep garment-accent footer with exact hotline `0989 353 247` and domain `mayaodongphuc.com.vn`.
- Require consistent garment design across all scene panels.
- Reject support windows that are merely identical duplicate crops. With the default two-image limit, reused source images are acceptable only when the crops and visual roles are clearly distinct.
- Reject AI-rendered catalog typography when deterministic composition is available.
