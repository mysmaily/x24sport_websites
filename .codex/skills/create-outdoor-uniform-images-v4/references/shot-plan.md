# V4 default scene plan

The default deliverable is exactly two accepted photographic scene images plus one 3:2 catalog board assembled from those images. Generate no more than two AI scene images unless the user explicitly asks for more.

| Image | Role | Default cast | Direction |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Image 1 | Primary lifestyle/product scene | 4 | Shirt-first outdoor group, relaxed but commercial, clear fronts; optional one back view. Use as product hero/gallery image. |
| Image 2 | Supporting lifestyle/content scene | 4–6 | Friendly interaction, walking, light team activity, picnic, or outing moment; different formation, crop, and gaze from Image 1. Use as product content image. |

For the catalog board, use Image 1 as the hero and Image 2 plus a distinct crop from Image 1 or Image 2 for the lower supporting windows.

## Variation

Randomly vary setting and composition between runs. A good V4 output may combine lakeside lineup + picnic, resort lawn + walking, or wooded outing + team interaction. Do not lock every run to one exact background or group count.

## Casting

Default to believable Vietnamese/Southeast Asian adults around 20–35 with distinct faces, varied height/build/hair and natural expressions. Mixed gender unless the brief says otherwise.

## Framing

Keep garments visually dominant. Hero should generally be head-to-thigh/knee. Supporting windows may be slightly wider, but avoid excessive empty ground and tiny shirts.

## One or several garment designs

One design: repeat consistently. Coordinated colorways: assign intentionally. Front/back pair: show both naturally. Different designs: label A/B/C in prompts and never blend features.
