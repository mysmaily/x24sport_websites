# Approved Output Contract

## Brand Constants

- Logo asset: `../assets/mayaodongphuc-logo.png`
- Garment text: user-supplied child/class/school text, a short placeholder from `short-shirt-text-dictionary.md`, or fallback `Đồng Phục X24`
- Hotline: `0982 254 458`
- Website: `mayaodongphuc.com.vn`
- Cast: believable Vietnamese children in preschool or primary-school age, with natural anatomy, age-appropriate expression, and safe supervision cues when the scene needs them
- Brand impression: friendly, safe, easy to order for schools/classes/clubs, production-capable

The supplied garment controls the palette. Mayaodongphuc brand colors are secondary campaign accents and must not recolor the product. Remove all supplied logos, names, class/school marks, event marks and original text from the garment while preserving non-brand decorative design. Put the user-supplied text, one short child-safe placeholder phrase, or fallback `Đồng Phục X24` on the garment in a readable print area. For children's uniforms, prefer rounded, bright, simple typography and gentle school-themed icons. Keep the shirt as the product; bottoms and accessories are styling only and must not be described as included.

## Main Image Info Treatment

Apart from the garment text, main images may show only short commercial feature/contact copy from this set:

- `THOÁNG MÁT`
- `DỄ MẶC`
- `IN TÊN - LOGO LỚP`
- `MAY NHANH SỐ LƯỢNG LỚN`
- `0982 254 458`

Do not force every main image to show every item. Pick 2-4 feature/contact items according to the composition and negative space. Use simple icons only when they help scanning. Do not show the website on main.

The information treatment must vary across a product batch so the website does not look like the same template repeated. Acceptable treatments include:

- small floating feature badges near safe corners;
- a compact translucent corner panel;
- soft sticker labels that follow the scene palette;
- a short vertical stack along a clean side edge;
- a curved ribbon or partial rail that does not span the full width;
- a small phone/contact chip separated from feature badges.

Avoid the repeated full-width dark-blue bottom rail shown in older benchmarks. A bottom rail is allowed only as an occasional variant when it fits the scene, stays visually light, and does not become the default batch pattern.

## Composition Limits

### Main

- Full-canvas photography.
- Use preschool, primary-school or safe outing context rather than teen class, corporate outing or fashion context.
- Keep logo around 12-15% of shorter edge.
- Main information treatment uses at most 12% of image area and must not cover the garment design or crop children awkwardly.
- Vary treatment placement, shape, opacity, and accent color across products. Do not reuse the same full-width footer/rail structure across a catalog batch.
- Prefer three to five children and at least three readable shirt fronts.
- A three-quarter back or full back can replace one shirt front when it clearly reveals the back artwork.
- Direct-to-camera gaze should feel like a warm school/product photo.

### Image 2

- Square 1:1.
- Four to eight children in a distinct classroom, schoolyard, playground, club or outing setting.
- Include at least one clear shirt back unless the product is explicitly front-only.
- Scenes may be lively but must remain supervised, calm, safe and age-appropriate.
- No catalog titles, feature paragraphs, website or factory footer.
- Keep only Mayaodongphuc campaign logo and garment wordmark unless the user asks for a no-logo clean image.

### Catalog

- 5:4 by default.
- Photography remains at least 60% of perceived visual weight.
- Use Version B as the hero scene and avoid using the main crop alone.
- Information field should be softly integrated, not a hard opaque sidebar.
- Show four useful garment details: fabric, collar/seam, front print/panel, and back artwork or sleeve when supported by the source.
